# IMDEX5 threat model (READ FIRST)

For auditors. `src/Imdex5.sol` is launch 2343's audited and deployed code (`identity-md-launches/launch-2343-the-imdex-5` at `c100c77`) with the fixes audit 705dd677 asked for, and the two fixes the independent review of commit a578235 asked for (both medium, `test/Imdex5Payments.t.sol`): a coin payment runs in a reverting self-call (`paySelf`, `safeTransfer` under `PAY_GAS`), so a coin that moves the funds and then returns false is rolled back before the amount is recorded as owed and is never paid twice (review eade2f07); and `redeemToImd` pays the in-kind part of a failed or short sale after the PoolManager is locked again, not inside its unlock, so a held coin whose transfer touches the manager cannot revert every holder's IMD exit (review e3e0815c). The audit of this launch's code (one low, three informational, none medium or above) changed no code: each finding is reproduced in `test/Imdex5DocumentedLimits.t.sol` and listed under the accepted limits below. The 705dd677 fixes: a rebalance or `redeemToImd` swap that takes its input and delivers nothing reverts `NothingOut` inside `swapSelf` and is skipped (medium); a rebalance swap that delivers less than half its input's value at the pool price before it reverts `PoorFill` and is skipped (the same medium, closed against a dust payout); a mint's exact-output residual buy is exempt from both, so its shortfall is charged at the pool price as before; the swap amount is converted with `SafeCast` (low); `mint` refuses `minShares` above `shares` before any buy (low). Launch 2343 itself was launch 1668's code with the 886e7c22 fixes and the 3bd5e1aa fix (a residual whose buy reverts fails the mint instead of being settled at a movable spot price). Everything else is unchanged.

## Actors

| Actor | Can | Trusted to |
|---|---|---|
| Anyone (minter) | `mint` with IMD | nothing; may be a contract, may re-enter through a hook, may front-run |
| Anyone (holder) | `redeem`, `redeemToImd`, transfer shares | nothing |
| Anyone (caller) | `onOracleResult` with the panel's signed answer, then `rebalance` to apply it | nothing beyond submitting a valid signature |
| IMD oracle panel | signs the list of the five largest IMD-paired coins (EIP-712, `IdentityMD Oracle` v2, signer `0x5598…2982`) | at least `MIN_AGREED` = 4 agree on the list |
| Coin pools and hooks (Uniswap v4) | price, liquidity, hooks that can revert, burn gas, re-enter or tax | nothing |
| Coins | may tax on transfer (SwarmCity 2%), return false, revert, burn gas | nothing |

No owner, admin, role, pause or upgrade.

## Invariants (must hold on every path)

1. Every IMD the contract holds is in `feeImd`, `idleImd`, or a running call's accounting; nothing is unaccounted.
2. `held[coin]` equals the coin balance the contract actually holds (what arrived, not what was asked).
3. A mint never issues more shares than the coins it added back per existing share (no dilution of holders); with `minShares > 0` it never issues fewer than `minShares`.
4. A redeem pays each holder their pro-rata share minus the 0.50% fee; the last holder is paid in full, by `redeem` and by `redeemToImd` alike.
5. A failed or short swap never leaves value with the caller that belongs to holders.
6. Only an oracle answer signed by the panel, for this contract, unexpired, with at least four agreeing members in the signed attestation itself, and not replayed, can change the list.

## Deliberately not findings (accepted, documented)

- A coin in the list whose pool dies blocks `mint` (`BuyFailed`) until the pool works again or every holder redeems so `held` reaches zero; `redeem` and `redeemToImd` still work. No `rebalance` drops it: step (1) can only sell it through the same dead pool, so the sale is skipped, and step (2) keeps every leaver with `held > 0` as a residual, whose reverting buy blocks `mint` the same way (next item). Whoever can make a listed coin's pool or token revert can therefore keep `mint` closed without holding a share; this is the documented tradeoff of audit 3bd5e1aa, since settling a reverted buy at a spot price was worse (review 87c33a5f, low: the earlier wording "until a rebalance drops it" was wrong; `test/Imdex5DocumentedLimits.t.sol`).
- A residual (a coin that left the list and could not yet be sold) is bought by `mint` like any holding. A residual whose pool trades but fills short (a transfer tax, liquidity that ran out) does not block `mint`: the shortfall is paid at the price the pool just traded at into the holders' idle IMD. Holders keep their value; they hold a little less of that coin and a little more IMD. That price is the one the mint's own buy moved to, so it is at or above the price before the buy; moving it first costs the mover what the buy returns. A residual whose pool rejects the index's buy outright (a dead pool, a hook that reverts, burns gas or leaves a delta, a swap that needs more than `SWAP_GAS`) is a failed buy: `mint` reverts `BuyFailed` and takes nothing, as for a dead list coin, until the pool works again or a `rebalance` sells the residual; `redeem` and `redeemToImd` keep working. Settling such a buy at the pool's spot price (audit 3bd5e1aa) let a minter move that price first and take the residual from holders for pool fees and gas.
- A residual pool with no liquidity left in the buy direction: Uniswap v4 fills what it can and leaves the price at the limit, so the shortfall's charge is astronomical and the mint reverts `TooExpensive`. A mint can request at most what the residual's pool can deliver (audit be08f42a, low; documented, not redesigned).
- Residual and idle charges use the requested `shares`; a list coin's short fill reduces `minted` but not the residual fraction bought or charged, so the minter overpays for that fraction by the short fill's ratio and the excess stays with the holders, as any coin bought beyond the issued fraction does (audit 4acb1b2e, low; documented, not redesigned; `minShares` lets the buyer refuse a short fill).
- A rebalance swap, or a `redeemToImd` sale, that takes its input and delivers nothing reverts `NothingOut` inside `swapSelf`, so the caller skips it (audit 705dd677, medium). A rebalance swap must also deliver at least half its input's value at the pool price before it, or it reverts `PoorFill` and is skipped: a hook or tax that keeps more than half is a loss, not a fee. A hook may still keep up to half on a rebalance swap; that is the accepted bound. A mint's exact-output residual buy is exempt from both, so its shortfall is charged as above and mint stays open.
- `PoorFill` and the 1% sqrt-price limit are measured against the pool's price when `rebalance()` runs, not against the price before the caller's own transaction. Anyone may call `rebalance()` once a list is submitted, so a caller who moves a list coin's price up (or a leaver's down) first has the index trade at the moved price, and the fill is booked (audit 9f216ca3, low; documented, not redesigned). The loss is bounded by the move and by the limit: a rebalance swap trades at most what moves the sqrt price 1%, about 1% of the pool's reserve per coin per weekly rebalance, and a 44% pump costs about 30% of that one buy's coins (`test/Imdex5DocumentedLimits.t.sol`); on mainnet the 1.25% pool fee each way makes the sandwich net-negative for the mover, so it is griefing at a cost, not extraction. A reference price the caller cannot set (a TWAP) is a redesign.
- A limited sale sells about 1% of the pool's coin reserve per rebalance (a 1% sqrt-price move on a constant-product curve), so a leaver larger than that stays as a residual and is sold again by each weekly rebalance until it is gone; meanwhile every `mint` buys it, every `redeem` pays it and every `rebalance` spends up to `SWAP_GAS` on it (audit 0a7c3762, info). Residuals are the normal state after a list change.
- `ask()` sets `lastAsk` when it opens a request, so a request that fails, is closed after two days or returns an answer `submit` cannot use costs the whole week and its 0.75 IMD; the next `ask` waits for the interval (audit 18ca8bf9, info). "Weekly at most" is the rule.
- The version 2 Intake forwards every payment to its `payTo` in the request call and holds nothing, so no IMD comes back on a failed request and invariant 1 holds across one (audit e2325942, info: asked whether a refund would strand IMD; its source is verified on Sourcify with a full match, and it has no refund path).
- A list of fewer than four coins is invested only a quarter of total value per coin: step (3) skips the trim below four coins, but step (4) still caps each buy at `total / WEIGHT_DIVISOR`, so with `k < 4` coins at most `k / 4` of the index is in coins and the rest stays idle IMD that no later rebalance to the same list spends (`submit` accepts one to five launches, so a week in which fewer than four pools hold 100 IMD produces this). `mint` keeps the idle proportion, and `redeem` and `redeemToImd` return idle IMD at par, so nothing is lost; the index holds cash until a list of four or more coins arrives (review 2ff07688, low; documented, not redesigned; `test/Imdex5DocumentedLimits.t.sol`).
- `submit` takes the answer whole: one entry whose launch no registry records, or that lacks a `token` or `hook` artifact (a contracts-only launch whose ERC-20 someone gave a 100 IMD pool), reverts `NotRecorded` or `ArtifactMissing` for the list, and the week and its 0.75 IMD are spent (the request is already closed; `ask` waits for the interval). While the panel keeps ranking such a launch the list stays as it is and 0.75 IMD leaks per week; `mint`, `redeem` and `redeemToImd` keep working (review e7a4430f, low; documented, not redesigned: skipping entries would adopt a list the panel did not sign, and the question's exclusion clause is the oracle's, not this contract's; `test/Imdex5Revised.t.sol`).
- A coin that cannot be paid (its transfer reverts, returns false, burns `PAY_GAS` or has no code) is recorded as owed and claimed later with `claim`, which uses `safeTransfer` and pays once. A coin that moves the funds and only then returns false is rolled back by `paySelf`'s revert, so it is owed, not paid and owed (review eade2f07, medium, fixed). The in-kind part of `redeemToImd` is paid after the unlock, through the same `_pay`, so a coin's transfer can neither reach the unlocked manager nor revert the exit (review e3e0815c, medium, fixed).
- Small leftovers (rounding, coins bought beyond the issued fraction) stay with existing holders.
- SwarmCity's 2% transfer tax costs a minter about 2% of shares (the floor at 97% passes).
- A round trip (mint then redeem) costs about 3-5% (two 0.50% fees, pool fees, the tax).
- `maxImdIn` caps payment only; `minShares` is the share guarantee.

## Severity scale

Critical: holders' or minters' funds can be taken or frozen by anyone. High: funds lost under realistic conditions or an invariant broken. Medium: loss needs unusual but reachable conditions, or a core function can be blocked by someone else. Low: rounding, gas, clarity. Info: notes.

## Tests

187 unit and fuzz tests against mocks (`forge test`), including the review's fixes (`test/Imdex5Payments.t.sol`: a coin that moves the funds and returns false is owed once and never paid twice, from `redeem` and from the in-kind part of `redeemToImd`; every failure mode is owed once and nothing moves; a held coin that touches the manager from its transfer is paid in kind after the unlock and every holder's IMD exit works; in-kind payments match the redeem rule for any shares and any set of dead pools), the documented limits above (`test/Imdex5DocumentedLimits.t.sol`: a pool moved before a rebalance is traded at the moved price and booked, within the stated bound; a limited sale sells about 1% of the reserve; a failed or closed request costs the week; a failed request leaves every IMD accounted; a two-coin list leaves about half the value idle; a dead list coin is not dropped by a rebalance), the rebalance payout rules (`test/Imdex5RebalanceNothingOut.t.sol`: a rebalance buy or sell that pays nothing is skipped; a buy that pays 1 wei is skipped and one that pays 60% trades), the floor (`test_sliverFillBelowFloorRevertsAndRollsBack`, `test_fullFillPassesFloor`) and the residual attacks (`test/Imdex5ResidualSpot.t.sol`: a dumped residual whose buy reverts cannot be minted at spot, at any price; a residual hook that burns gas or leaves a delta fails the mint; a dumped pool that trades is bought in full and dilutes nobody; a short fill is charged at least its fair value). 8 mainnet fork tests (`test/Fix3*.fork.t.sol`, skipped without `--fork-url`): mint and redeem at 10, 100 and 1,000 IMD; rebalance to three, four and the same five coins; floor passes at 97% and cancels at 99%; an oversized buy is cancelled by the floor.
